FAQs

How do we get to the venue?

There will be a wedding bus to take you to and from Kato Paphos and Liopetro.

For timings please see the Timeline.

Where should we stay?

We recommend the Kato Paphos area – see our suggested hotels on the Accommodation page.

Can I bring a plus one or children?

We unfortunately cannot accommodate plus ones or children beyond those named on the wedding invitation. Thank you for understanding!

What should I wear?

The bridal party will be in fuchsia, so please avoid this colour if possible! Ladies: think colourful / floral midi or maxi dresses. Gentlemen: suit and tie, kindly no shorts.

More on the Dress Code page.

What's the food?

A traditional Cypriot BBQ – see the Food page. Please let us know about any dietary requirements on the RSVP form.

When do I need to RSVP by?

We ask that all guests RSVP by Friday 2nd June 2028.
